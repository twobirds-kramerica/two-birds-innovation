[age:AI Q3 2026] The Automation of Automation

September 14, 2026 | Builders Club, Kitchener, ON | Sam Hebeisen (mindmodel.ai) & Colleagues

Event: age:AI Q3 2026 Briefing
Location: Builders Club, Kitchener, ON
Lead Presenter: Sam Hebeisen (mindmodel.ai) & Colleagues
Format: Chronological Media & Session Notes
Topic 01

Data Valuation & Human Capture

The session opened with an exploration of how AI training datasets are expanding into every corner of human activity. As model developers exhaust standard web crawl data, attention has shifted to high-resolution capture of human labor, expert domain knowledge, and specialized workflows.

Key highlights from this discussion centered on the economic framing of human output:

  • Training sets everywhere: Continuous background capture across software tools, creative platforms, and operational workflows converts tacit human knowledge into structured model weights.
  • Hourly-rate framing vs. data valuation: Traditional labor pricing measures time spent ($/hour), but model training repositions work as one-time data extraction. Once captured into training corpora, the marginal cost of reproducing expert problem-solving drops toward zero.
  • The shift to data royalty & capture models: Discussion on how individual practitioners and organizations can evaluate the true enterprise value of their proprietary operational data before licensing or exposing it to model trainers.
Topic 02

Biological Wetware

Moving beyond silicon architectures, the talk examined frontier biological computing and bio-hybrid neural systems. As compute energy demands scale exponentially, biological wetware presents extreme energy efficiency and novel learning paradigms.

Key case studies highlighted during this segment included:

  • Cortical Labs' DishBrain: Biological human and rodent neurons cultured on high-density microelectrode arrays (HD-MEAs), demonstrating real-time adaptive learning by playing the classic arcade game Pong.
  • Project CETI (Cetacean Translation Initiative): Utilizing bio-acoustic sensor arrays, underwater hydrophones, and deep generative models to decipher sperm whale click communication (codas) and inter-species linguistics.
  • Bio-silicon integration: How biological neural substrate interfaces offer sub-watt processing efficiencies compared to multi-kilowatt GPU clusters for closed-loop sensory-motor control.
Topic 03

Agentic Infrastructure

The core theme of the event focused on giving autonomous AI agents "hands" — moving from passive chat interfaces to active tooling, API execution, and end-to-end task completion across complex digital environments.

Key highlights and live demonstrations included:

  • Tooling & Protocols (Make / n8n / MCP): Connecting LLM reasoning engines directly to real-world workflows using low-code integration platforms like Make, open-source automation via n8n, and Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) for standardizing local and remote tool execution.
  • Devin Bulk-Barn-Style Multi-Step Demo: A live demonstration using autonomous coding agent Devin to parse unstructured physical inventory catalogues, automate bin-level item mapping, and generate production code without human intervention.
  • Bowl Builder Automation: Case study on custom order automation for multi-ingredient fast-casual dining, demonstrating automated ticket routing and ingredient optimization.
  • Diner Automation Survey Stats: Benchmark metrics on labor replacement vs augmentation, service speed gains, and ROI numbers from regional quick-service hospitality deployments. Mention of emerging gig/human-in-the-loop fallback models like Rentahuman.
Referenced Tools & Protocols: Make · n8n · Devin (Cognition) · Rentahuman
Topic 04

Physical Automation

Bridging software intelligence with hardware execution, the session featured a live physical robotics demonstration using the Unitree Go2 Pro quadruped robot dog.

Key demonstration takeaways:

  • Unitree Go2 Pro Quadruped: Demonstrating real-time terrain navigation, obstacle detection via onboard 3D LiDAR, dynamic payload balancing, and acrobatic maneuvers including an inline backflip.
  • Commercial deployment readiness: Discussion of falling hardware cost curves (sub-$3,000 quadruped platforms) enabling practical commercial applications in industrial facility inspection, security patrols, and last-mile logistics.
Topic 05

Realtime AI Avatar Demo

An interactive live demonstration showcased sub-second, multi-modal conversational avatars capable of natural real-time dialogue and dynamic multi-language translation.

Key aspects of the demo:

  • French-language realtime conversational avatar: Live interaction in French demonstrating seamless sub-200ms latency between speech input, reasoning generation, facial expression synthesis, and synchronized lip movement.
  • Enterprise applications: Low-friction deployment in customer service kiosks, bilingual public services, and automated video communications without pre-recorded voice talents.
Topic 06

Waterloo-Region School Board AI Policy Discussion

The agenda turned to local governance and institutional readiness, highlighting ongoing policy initiatives within Waterloo Region school boards (WRDSB / WCDSB) regarding generative AI adoption in education.

Core discussion themes:

  • Classroom AI Guidelines & Governance: Developing clear boundaries between permitted AI assist tools and academic dishonesty, while building digital literacy across K-12 curricula.
  • Equitable Access & Privacy Safeguards: Ensuring student data protection, avoiding non-consensual data harvest by commercial LLM vendors, and providing equal access to advanced AI tools across diverse socio-economic student populations.
  • Teacher Enablement: Practical professional development strategies for educators to streamline lesson planning and administrative assessment using safe AI platforms.
Topic 07

Audience Temperature-Check / Four-Questions Poll

An interactive live polling segment engaged attendees to capture room sentiment across four critical dimensions regarding local AI adoption and economic expectations.

The four survey topics evaluated:

  • 1. Operational AI Integration Depth: To what extent are local organizations actively deploying LLMs and automated workflows in daily production?
  • 2. Governance & Safety Readiness: Are formal risk frameworks and data privacy guardrails keeping pace with rapid tool deployment?
  • 3. 12-24 Month Economic Impact: What degree of workforce displacement or productivity expansion do local founders and leaders anticipate?
  • 4. Autonomous Agent Sentiment: How comfortable are decision-makers delegating autonomous decision-making power to AI agents without manual oversight?
Topic 08

Epoch AI Compute & Data-Center Growth Discussion

The briefing concluded with a detailed examination of AI compute scaling trajectories, drawing from recent empirical data compiled by Epoch AI.

Key takeaways from the scaling analysis:

  • Exponential Compute Curves: Analyzing cumulative training FLOPS and the physical hardware expansion required to sustain frontier AI progress through 2026-2030.
  • Data Center Infrastructure Bottlenecks: Electrical grid capacity constraints, gigawatt-scale campus development, cooling requirements, and nuclear power purchase agreements powering frontier AI clusters.
  • Data Saturation & Synthetic Frontier: The transition from human text consumption to synthetic data generation and self-play environments as web data limits are reached.
